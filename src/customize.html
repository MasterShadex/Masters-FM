⚡ Quick Settings ▼

The fastest way to change the overall look. Adjust everything at once, then dive into individual sections below if you want to fine-tune any element.

COLOR AND SIZE
Accent color

Sets the main accent color for every element that hasn't been manually overridden. Click "Use accent" next to any per-element color picker to make that element follow this master.

Overall size
100%

Scales the whole card up or down. 100 percent is the standard size; lower shrinks it, higher grows it.

Text size
100%

Scales every piece of text (title, artist, label, timestamps) at once. Per-element text sizes stay relative -- the title stays larger than the artist.

EFFECTS
Glow

Master switch for every glow effect on the card. Turn off for a flat, clean look without changing any per-element glow settings.

Animations

Master switch for every animation (spinning border, slide-in, marquee scrolling, spectrum bars). Clock time updates always stay smooth.

🎨 Themes ▼

One-click color schemes — each theme changes the entire look: colors, fonts, glow, and spectrum style. Your custom tweaks are still there; pick a theme, then fine-tune anything you like.

⚙️ General ▼

Global settings that affect the entire overlay — opacity makes the whole widget more transparent so it blends into your stream background.

Overall transparency
100%

How see-through the whole overlay is. 100 = fully solid.

📐 Layout (advanced) ▼

Drag-and-drop the position of every element on the card. Pick a template to start, then click "Edit" to drag elements around in the live preview. Off by default — your existing preset layout is preserved until you opt in.

Use custom layout

Show / Lock Elements

Left toggle = visible. Right toggle (🔒) = lock so the element can't be dragged or resized in the preview.
