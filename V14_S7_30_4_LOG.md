  title/artist scroll pause, autogain, response time, fps, smoothness,
  glow color1, glow color2

  first `.sub-label` divider

V2 SETTINGS_CONFIG's `advanced: true` flag (Stage 7.28) already encodes
all these. Stage 7.28's renderAllControls Option A consolidation routes
them to a separate Advanced supercat at render time. Item 2 unwinds
that: keep natural supercat, add `data-advanced` attribute via
renderControl, gate visibility via body class.
